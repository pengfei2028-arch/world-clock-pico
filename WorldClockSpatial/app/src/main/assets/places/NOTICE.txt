South China Sea geographic search supplement / 南海主要岛礁搜索目录

This is a separate natural-feature browsing catalogue, not an administrative-city or jurisdiction database.
65 named features, compiled 2026-11-17. IDs 900000000001..900000000065 are stable project-local identifiers,
not GeoNames IDs. Do not renumber them: world-clock bookmarks and personal photo albums use these strings.
The original cities/catalog.tsv, city pin coordinates and timezone history are unchanged.

The NUS table's duplicated Subi Reef coordinate was rejected. The catalogue uses an independently checked
OSM position for 渚碧礁; no claim is made that any source is error-free or survey-grade.

ARCHIPELAGO: representative browsing point, often at sea, not the location of a city.
ISLAND/CAY: dry-land representative point where a usable coastline was available.
REEF: reef-flat/atoll reference point, possibly underwater; a Chinese name ending in 岛 or 沙洲 does not
assert that its entire reference polygon is dry land. No coastline, boundary or sovereignty claim is encoded.
The +08:00 clock is an explicit application reference offset; it is not a legal-timezone or historical-DST claim.
population=0 means population is not supplied, not a population measurement.

This list covers major named features, not every named rock, bank or reef in the South China Sea.
Coordinates use WGS84 latitude/longitude. Mesh placement must use coordinates/earth_surface.bin,
not a guessed world-space direction. Name search and placement require no network connection.
