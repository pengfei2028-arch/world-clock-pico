Island texture supplement / V105 non-commercial release

Prepared 2026-11-17. This build replaces only selected blocks/pixels of the
existing Full/Shared Day texture. Texture dimensions, resource count, shaders,
night lights, normal map, specular map, clouds and globe geometry are unchanged.
Small islands retain their geographic scale; a feature may be smaller than a
pixel at the existing global resolution. This is not a navigation/chart product.
